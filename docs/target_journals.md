## Recommendation
- **First choice: RESS or PSEP.** If you can make uncertainty + open-set the headline → **RESS** (highest IF, cleanest fit). If you frame around process safety and want reviewers already fluent in TEP → **PSEP**.
- **If a reviewer says "too incremental for a safety journal,"** pivot the framing to AI-method and target **EAAI**.
- **For the explicit Springer requirement**, lead with **Journal of Intelligent Manufacturing** (better scope fit than NCA for fault diagnosis).

## Also viable — but verify quartile per category before committing
- **Computers & Chemical Engineering** (Elsevier, IF ~4.4): the natural disciplinary home, but it is **Q1 in "Chemical Engineering (miscellaneous)" yet Q2 in "Computer Science Applications"** — borderline against a strict Q1 rule. Use only if the target category is the chem-eng one.
- **Journal of Process Control** (Elsevier, IF ~4–5): strong fit for the 6B soft-sensor/optimization paper; confirm its current quartile in your submission category first.

## Fit rationale (why these five, not generic AI journals)
Your contribution is simultaneously (a) a *process-safety* result, (b) an *AI-methods* result, and (c) a *reliability/uncertainty* result. RESS, PSEP, and EAAI each own one of those three axes at Q1; the two Springer venues cover the AI/manufacturing axis. Pure-ML megajournals (e.g., ESWA) would accept the method but bury the petrochemical significance that makes the work distinctive.

## Strategic note
Match the *framing of the abstract and introduction* to the chosen journal's axis — the same experiments can be sold to any of the three axes. Draft the paper once, then produce a journal-specific intro/positioning. Decide the target **before** writing the intro, not after.
